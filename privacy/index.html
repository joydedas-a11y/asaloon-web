Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Asaloon respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use the Asaloon mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

Asaloon may process information that you or your salon enter into the application, including:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, when provided
  • Salon name and salon information
  • Staff information
  • Customer names and phone numbers
  • Appointment information
  • Services and products
  • Billing and payment records
  • Inventory information
  • Business reports and operational information

We may also process limited technical information necessary to operate and improve the application, such as device information, application version and error information.

2. Customer Information

Asaloon allows salon businesses to store information about their customers. The salon using Asaloon is responsible for ensuring it has the right to collect and use its customers' information.

We do not sell customer information. We do not sell customer lists to advertisers.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide Asaloon features
  • Manage appointments
  • Create and maintain customer records
  • Create bills and payment records
  • Manage products and stock
  • Generate reports
  • Synchronize salon information where applicable
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain security
  • Diagnose errors
  • Improve the application

4. Device Permissions

Asaloon may request optional device permissions when required for a feature.

Contacts

Access may be requested when you choose to select or import a contact. Asaloon does not access contacts unless you use a feature that requires contact access.

Camera

Camera access may be used for features such as barcode scanning or capturing an image when you choose to use those features.

Photos

Photo access may be requested only when you choose an image or use a feature requiring access to your photo library.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Microphone or speech recognition access may be requested for optional voice-input features. Voice input is not required to use the core application.

Notifications

Notification permission may be requested to provide relevant application notifications.

5. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

If a salon chooses to connect WhatsApp or another third-party service, information required to provide that integration may be processed by the relevant third-party provider according to its own terms and privacy policy.

Asaloon does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only:

  • With service providers required to operate Asaloon
  • When you explicitly request or enable an integration
  • When required by applicable law
  • When necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service

Service providers are expected to process information only for the purpose of providing their services.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide Asaloon, meet legitimate business requirements and comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Account Deletion

Asaloon provides an in-app account deletion option. Users may permanently request deletion of their Asaloon account from within the mobile application.

Account access ends when the deletion process is initiated in accordance with the application's deletion process. Certain information may be retained temporarily where required for security, legal, fraud-prevention or compliance purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Asaloon is a business application intended for salon businesses and their authorized users. It is not designed for use by children.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Asaloon or applicable requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@asaloon.com
